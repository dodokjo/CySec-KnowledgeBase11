## The Attack Surface
"All of the potential vulnerabilities in a system"

### The Digital Surface (Software and Network)
- Public-facing websites / APIs
- Open network ports
- Unpatched OS vulnerabilities
- Third-party libraries

### The Physical Surface (Hardware and Access)
- Laptops, mobile devices, USB drives, IoT sensors
- Open ports
- Physical addresses of servers

### The Human Surface
- Susceptibility to phishing
- Weak password habits
- Insider threats
- Third-party vendor access

## Malware Mechanisms
- **Worm:** self-propagates across machines and networks
- **Trojan:** hides inside apparently legitimate software
- **Ransomware:** encrypts data and demands payment
- **Persistence:** survives via services, tasks or rootkits
- **Command and control (C2):** remote control of compromised machines

### Detection
- Signatures catch known samples
- Behavior catches new ones

## Cyber Defence
"Acting in anticipation to oppose an attack through cyber and cognitive domains"

- **Anticipation:** identify vulnerabilities, monitor threat intelligence, intercept adversaries
- **Cyber Domain:** secure technical infrastructure using threat-hunting, deception, [[honeypots]]
- **Cognitive Domain:** protect human decision-making from social engineering and disinformation

### What Computer Security Involves
- Preventing what you can
- Detecting what you cannot prevent
- Responding, containing and recovering when it happens

## Security Goals (The CIA Triad)
- Confidentiality
- Integrity
- Availability

## Threats and Attacks

### Categories of Computer Security Threats
- Unauthorized access
- Unauthorized modification
- Unauthorized disclosure
- Denial of authorized access
- Forgery
- Repudiation: where the integrity of an asset can be disputed
- Spoofing: pretending to be legitimate

### Forms of Attacks
- DoS
- Modify information
- Physical destruction
- Infrastructure attack
- Ransomware
- Masquerade as victim (e.g. credit card fraud)
- Add files
- Corporate espionage
- Information warfare

## Definitions

### Attack Surface:
- All of the potential vulnerabilities in a system
- Context: split into digital, physical and human surfaces

### Cyber Defence:
- Acting in anticipation to oppose an attack through cyber and cognitive domains

### CIA Triad:
- The three core security goals: Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability

### Worm:
- Malware that self-propagates across machines and networks

### Trojan:
- Malware hidden inside apparently legitimate software

### Ransomware:
- Malware that encrypts data and demands payment

### Persistence:
- How malware survives reboots/removal, via services, scheduled tasks or rootkits

### Command and Control (C2):
- Remote control of compromised machines by the attacker

### Repudiation:
- A threat where the integrity of an asset (or who did an action) can be disputed

### Spoofing:
- Pretending to be a legitimate user, system or source
